### Summary
I contributed to my fluid mechanical dissection of a Gadabout first by participating in the dissection of the device and identifying key components that I learned about in MAE 3230 such as a gear pump and hydraulic lines. I then determined the mechanical advantage created by the incompressible hydraulic fluid pressurizing a piston with a much larger surface area than the hydraulic lines. I determined the dissipated pressure through the hydraulic lines with the Haaland Equation and determined the rating for the Gadabout.
