Hi, my name is Raquel Sweet! I am a Senior at Cornell University majoring in Mechanical Engineering and minoring in Business. I’m originally from San Francisco, California, and I’m passionate about pursuing a career in aerospace that advances innovation and sustainability. I spent the summer of 2026 as a Engine Systems Intern at Boom Supersonic. At my internship I designed fluid distribution networks for water coolant, compressed air, nitrogen, and hydrogen, supplying 4 skids and a blade tip timing cooling system for Sprint Core, Boom's supersonic engine demonstrator. I also designed and built weather station to capture real-time atmospheric data, engineering it to withstand winds up to 115 mph. At Cornell, I’ve been involved in hands-on projects like Design Build Fly and the Magnus Ultralight Research Project, where I’ve worked on aircraft design, structural analysis, and prototyping. These experiences have deepened my interest in flight and given me the skills to connect classroom theory with real-world engineering. Beyond academics, I am in a sketch comedy group, and I enjoy traveling. Looking ahead, I hope to combine my technical background with my passion and creativity to make meaningful contributions to the aerospace field.
